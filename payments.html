Payments & Vendor Services

An overview of University payment processes for vendors, students, guest speakers, participants, and non-employees, including PaymentWorks onboarding and common payment methods.

05
01 / Categories

Payment Categories

Review the requirements and processing guidelines for different types of university payments and vendor classifications.

01 Vendors

New Vendors

Organizations or individuals providing goods or services.

  • PaymentWorks registration
  • Submit tax documentation
  • Select ACH or mailed check
  • Record created prior to payment
02 Support

Student Stipends

Payments supporting participation in University activities.

  • Program participation
  • Research support
  • Training opportunities
  • Department approval
03 Speakers

Honoraria

Payments to guest speakers or presenters.

  • Guest lectures
  • Workshop presenters
  • Cultural presentations
  • Documentation required
04 Expenses

Reimbursements

Repayment for approved University expenses.

  • Original receipts
  • Business purpose
  • Supervisor approval
  • Processed via system
05 Invoicing

Invoices

Payments submitted by vendors for completed work.

  • Invoice required
  • Purchase Order as needed
  • Department approval
  • Accounts Payable processing
06 Incentives

Participant Payments

Payments to individuals participating in approved activities.

  • Research participants
  • Program incentives
  • Community engagement
  • Documentation required
