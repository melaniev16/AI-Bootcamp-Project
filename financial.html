Some purchases and payments require approval before the transaction occurs.

Prior approval requirements depend on the type of purchase, payment, funding source, and University process being used. When in doubt, obtain the required approval before making the purchase or committing University funds.

There are two common options: Jaggaer or SoftDocs / Student Resource Payment.

Option 1 — Jaggaer

Jaggaer can be used to pay both students and non-students/non-employees. The payee must first complete the University's onboarding process, and the onboarding must be approved by Purchasing & Contracts before the payment request can be processed.

Once the payment request is processed, payment may take up to two months to reach the recipient.

Option 2 — SoftDocs / Student Resource Payment

For eligible student payments, the SoftDocs Student Resource Payment method can generally get funds to the student much more quickly.

Important: If the student has an outstanding University balance, the payment may be applied to that balance instead of being paid directly to the student.

Take note: Before using a Student Resource Payment, consider whether the student has an outstanding University balance. If they do, the funds may be applied to that balance.
Quick comparison
  • Jaggaer: Can be used for students and non-employees; requires payee onboarding and Purchasing & Contracts approval; payment can take up to two months.
  • SoftDocs / Student Resource Payment: For eligible student payments; generally faster, but an outstanding University balance may absorb the payment.
